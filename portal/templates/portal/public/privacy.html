Privacy Policy

What we store. Your name, email, optional phone number, and the one student linked to your account. Teachers store student academic details (roll number, department, year, section) and meeting remarks they write after a PTM.

What we do not do. No advertising, no analytics tracking, no sharing of your data with anyone outside the college. Teacher remarks are private to the teacher and are never shown to parents or students.

Passwords. Demo passwords are stored plainly for this college project. In a real deployment they would be hashed.

Deletion. A teacher can delete a parent account, which removes the parent's data and frees the linked student.

Contact. For corrections, contact the class teacher who manages your records.
