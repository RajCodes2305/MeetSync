Book your parent-teacher meeting slot online

MeetSync lets parents book a meeting slot with the class teacher without phone calls or queues. The teacher publishes dates and times, you pick a free slot, and it is confirmed instantly. One booking per student per event.

{% if not user.is_authenticated %} Register as a parent Login {% endif %}
1. Pick your child

At registration you select your child once. The link is permanent, so every booking is always for your own child.

2. Choose a slot

The teacher creates a PTM event with dates and times. Free slots appear as chips; booked ones disappear immediately.

3. Show up

You get a confirmation with date, time and venue. Cancel or reschedule any time before the meeting.
